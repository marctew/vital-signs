{{ 'Edit split screen ' ~ item.name if split else 'Add a split screen' }}

Shows several content items at once on a grid: 2 wide by 4 tall for portrait screens, 4 wide by 2 tall for landscape. A page can cover one cell or a block of them, and keeps its own zoom and refresh interval. To make a cell rotate through several pages, put them in a playlist and place "playlist name (rotating)" on the grid. Modules such as Plex, service status and calendar, and rotating cells, can be set to disappear while they have nothing to show, so the other pages take their space. Sites that need a login may not stay signed in inside a split; test those before relying on them.
